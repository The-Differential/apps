Why Contact Capture?

📸

One Photo, Many Contacts

Snap a photo of a staff directory or sign-in sheet and extract every contact at once. No typing required.

🤖

AI-Powered Accuracy

Advanced AI reads and structures names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, and job titles automatically.

🏢

Smart Extension Dialing

Automatically converts short office extensions (e.g., "x5501") into full, dialable numbers based on your office prefix.

Smart Duplicate Detection

Finds existing contacts on your phone and lets you merge new details — like a job title or extension — without creating duplicates.

Built for Business Pros

Conference Leads — Scan attendee lists and badge sheets instantly.

How It Works

  1. Take a photo of any page with contact information.
  2. AI extracts names, numbers, emails, addresses, and titles.
  3. Review and edit each contact before saving.
  4. Save to your phone directly into your Contacts app.

Recent Scans: Your last 20 scans are saved locally so you can revisit and save contacts you skipped the first time. No account needed — your data stays on your device.
