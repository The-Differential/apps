Privacy Policy

Contact Scanner by The Differential

Last Updated: October 5, 2025

Introduction

Contact Scanner ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

1. Camera and Photo Library Access

  • Purpose: To capture or select images of contact sheets for processing
  • Storage: Images are temporarily processed and NOT permanently stored on our servers
  • Retention: Images are immediately discarded after contact extraction is complete

2. Contact Information Extracted from Images

  • What We Extract: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, job titles, and company information visible in your photos
  • How It's Used: Extracted information is presented to you for review and is only saved to your device's Contacts app with your explicit permission
  • Server Processing: Images are sent to our secure backend API (hosted on Vercel) for AI processing via Google Gemini AI

3. Contacts Permission

  • Purpose: To save extracted contact information to your device's native Contacts app
  • Control: You have full control over which contacts are saved. We only save contacts when you explicitly tap "Save" or "Add Contacts"

Third-Party Services

Google Gemini AI

We use Google's Gemini AI service to analyze images and extract contact information.

  • Data Sent: Images you choose to process through the app
  • Purpose: AI-powered optical character recognition and contact information extraction
  • Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Important: Your images are processed according to Google's privacy policies

Vercel Hosting

Our backend API is hosted on Vercel's infrastructure.

  • Data Transmitted: Images for processing, API requests
  • Purpose: Secure cloud infrastructure for our processing services
  • Vercel's Privacy Policy: https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy

How We Use Your Information

  1. Contact Extraction: Process images to identify and extract contact information using AI
  2. Contact Management: Save extracted contacts to your device's Contacts app (with your permission)
  3. App Functionality: Provide real-time processing updates and error handling

Data Security

Measures We Take:

  • ✅ HTTPS Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses secure HTTPS connections
  • ✅ No Permanent Storage: Images are NOT stored on our servers after processing
  • ✅ No User Accounts: We do not require user registration or store personal profiles
  • ✅ On-Device Storage: Extracted contacts are saved only to your device's local Contacts app

Important Security Notes:

  • We do NOT store your images or extracted contact data on our servers
  • We do NOT share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes
  • Processing is done in real-time and data is immediately discarded after extraction

Data Retention

  • Images: Deleted immediately after AI processing completes (typically 30-40 seconds)
  • Extracted Contacts: Only stored on YOUR device in the Contacts app, under your control
  • API Logs: Server logs containing request metadata (timestamps, image sizes) are retained for up to 30 days for debugging and error analysis. These logs do NOT contain your actual images or extracted contact information.

Medical/Health Information

IMPORTANT NOTICE: This app is designed for extracting professional contact information from directory sheets, often in medical facilities.

⚠️ HIPAA Compliance: This app is NOT HIPAA-compliant and should NOT be used to process Protected Health Information (PHI) or patient data.

⚠️ Intended Use: Only use this app to scan:

  • Professional contact directories
  • Business cards
  • Staff contact sheets
  • Department phone lists

❌ DO NOT use this app to scan:

  • Patient information
  • Medical records
  • Prescription information
  • Any documents containing sensitive health data

Your Rights and Choices

You Have the Right To:

  1. Delete Contacts: Remove any saved contacts from your device's Contacts app at any time
  2. Revoke Permissions: Disable camera and contacts access in your device settings
  3. Stop Using the App: Uninstall the app at any time, which removes all app data from your device

How to Control Your Data:

  • Camera Access: Settings → Contact Scanner → Camera → Toggle Off
  • Contacts Access: Settings → Contact Scanner → Contacts → Toggle Off
  • Delete Saved Contacts: Open Contacts app → Select contact → Delete

Children's Privacy

Contact Scanner is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International Users

This app processes data using servers located in the United States. By using Contact Scanner, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in the United States.

  • Displaying a notice in the app (for material changes)
